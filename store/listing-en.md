## App name

Deep Line

## Tagline

One breath. One line.

## Short description

A one-button freediving rhythm game for round Garmin watches.

## Full description

Descend the line. Equalize on rhythm. Take the tag. Turn cleanly. Kick back to
the surface before your FLOW runs out.

Deep Line is a compact one-button arcade game inspired by freediving. Every dive
starts beside the buoy and becomes a short timing challenge: follow equalization
cues on the way down, time the tag-and-turn cue at depth, then match your
kicks on the ascent and stay calm through the final glide.

Explore 15 dives from 10 m to an intentionally aspirational 150 m finale across
the Sea of Cortez, Visayan Sea, Red Sea, Atlantic Ocean and Greenland Sea. Each
region has its own water palette and depth-linked wildlife, including sea lions,
turtles, thresher sharks, manta rays, hammerheads, narwhals and orcas.

Features:

- One-button play with START/ENTER, plus touch controls on compatible watches
- 15 progressively deeper dives across five seas
- PERFECT, GOOD and MISSED timing with a persistent FLOW meter
- A single universal timing cue for the tag and turn
- One to three stars per completed dive; two stars unlock the next depth
- Animated freediver, buoy, ocean and region-appropriate wildlife
- Separate high-color AMOLED and palette-safe MIP presentation
- Fully offline: no account, network connection, sensors or permissions
- Local best scores and campaign progress

Deep Line is an arcade game. It is not a breath-hold trainer, dive computer,
medical device or safety tool. Never play it during a real dive or breath-hold
session.

## Controls

- START/ENTER or tap: select, equalize, tag and turn, and kick
- UP/DOWN or side taps: browse dives
- BACK: pause; while paused, resume the current dive
- Pause menu: Resume or Main Menu
