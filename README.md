# Deep Line

One breath. One line.

A one-button freediving rhythm game for round Garmin watches. Descend beside the
line, equalize on cue, take the tag, turn and return to the surface before FLOW
runs out. Explore 15 dives from 10 m to a fictional 150 m finale across five seas.

[Get Deep Line on Connect IQ](https://apps.garmin.com/apps/cd711127-7293-4b26-83e1-de9d8d26dd07) · [Website](https://addicted.sh/deep-line) · [Privacy](PRIVACY.md)

This README describes the payment-enabled update. Check the Connect IQ listing
for the version currently available for your watch.

## Free levels and the full game

The first three levels are free. A one-time purchase through KiezelPay gives
access to the remaining 12 levels. No subscription.

Purchase access and campaign progression are separate: finish each dive with
at least two stars to advance to the next depth.

- **FULL GAME** opens the purchase screen. Choose **BUY / RESTORE** and follow
  the code instructions on your phone.
- **LOCKED** means the previous dive still needs to be completed with two stars.
- **DIVE** means the selected level is ready to play.

You can cancel a purchase screen and keep playing the free levels. For purchase
restoration, follow [KiezelPay's existing-purchase flow](https://kzl.io/unlock).

Players updating from the free version with existing saved scores or campaign
progress retain full access locally. This retained access can be lost if the
app or its stored data is deleted.

## Features

- Fifteen progressively deeper dives across five seas
- Equalization, tag-and-turn and ascent timing with PERFECT, GOOD and MISSED cues
- One to three stars per dive, local best scores and saved campaign progress
- Physical-button and touch controls on supported devices
- Separate AMOLED and MIP graphics with animated ocean wildlife
- Offline gameplay; phone internet is needed for purchase and license checks

## Controls

- START / ENTER or tap: select, dive, equalize, take the tag and kick
- UP / DOWN or side taps: browse campaign levels
- BACK during a dive: pause; BACK while paused: resume
- Pause menu: Resume or Main Menu
- BACK on the purchase screen: return to free play

## Compatibility and privacy

See the Connect IQ listing for the currently supported watch models. The
expanded-device update includes fēnix 7 Pro Solar and additional fēnix, epix,
Forerunner, Venu, vívoactive and Instinct AMOLED models.

Progress and scores stay on the watch. Optional KiezelPay licensing uses the
Connect IQ Communications permission. Deep Line does not use health or location
data, advertising SDKs or analytics. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for the data
used to process and verify purchases.
